## 1) Macro Structure Checklist
- Does the typographic system clearly reflect content hierarchy?
- Is body text visually dominant over metadata and UI chrome?
- Are heading levels distinct and consistently spaced?
- Does the text block shape suit reading intent?
- Are margins and whitespace structural, not decorative residue?

## 2) Rhythm and Spacing Checklist
- Is line length in a comfortable range for the medium?
- Is leading sufficient for the chosen measure and face?
- Are paragraph transitions clear and consistent?
- Are block quotes, lists, tables, and notes rhythmically integrated?
- Are hyphenation and line breaks controlled and non-distracting?
- Are widows/orphans handled without mechanical artifacts?

## 3) Microtypography Checklist
- Are numeral styles appropriate (text vs titling/lining)?
- Are ligatures enabled where the face and language require them?
- Are quotation marks, apostrophes, and dashes used consistently?
- Are ranges and dimensions using proper symbols?
- Are ellipses, brackets, and parentheses style-consistent?
- Are accents and diacritics correct and visually balanced?

## 4) Typeface System Checklist
- Is the number of families minimal and role-driven?
- Does each face have a clear functional job?
- Do serif/sans pairings share internal structural harmony?
- Are bold, italic, and small-cap styles used intentionally?
- Are display faces supportive rather than dominant noise?
- Do non-Latin scripts integrate with optical balance?

## 5) Screen Delivery Checklist
- Is typography tested on common viewport widths?
- Is body text readable at realistic zoom settings?
- Are line lengths adjusted per breakpoint?
- Are rendering differences across browser/OS acceptable?
- Do interactive surfaces preserve readability under density?
- Are text tokens documented for engineering consistency?

## 6) Print/PDF Delivery Checklist
- Are choices validated against final paper/press assumptions?
- Are folios, running heads, and section markers clear?
- Are front matter and back matter visually balanced?
- Are tables/notes legible at final print size?
- Has final proof been reviewed after export/imposition?
- Have production-stage regressions been resolved?

## Severity Rubric for Reviews
- `P0`: Reading comprehension failure or severe ambiguity.
- `P1`: Structural hierarchy/rhythm issues that degrade core usability.
- `P2`: Noticeable polish and consistency defects.
- `P3`: Minor refinements with low functional risk.
