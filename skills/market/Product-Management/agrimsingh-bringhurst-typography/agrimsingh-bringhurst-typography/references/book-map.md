## Practical Scope by Chapter
- `1` Define typographic philosophy and first-principles behavior.
- `2` Control horizontal and vertical rhythm (measure, leading, paragraph logic, hyphenation).
- `3` Manage scale, numerals, ligatures, and restrained variation.
- `4` Build document structures (titles, headings, notes, tables, front/back matter).
- `5` Handle punctuation, dashes, brackets, quotes, diacritics, and symbol quality.
- `6` Choose, combine, and extend type systems responsibly.
- `7` Understand historical letterform traditions to avoid shallow stylistic borrowing.
- `8` Shape page geometry, text blocks, margins, and modular scales.
- `9` Integrate digital production realities (screen reading, QA loops, print follow-through).
- `10` Identify and name type categories accurately.

## How to Use This Map
1. Use chapters `1-2` for early layout strategy.
2. Use chapters `3-6` for type-system decisions.
3. Use chapters `5` and `9` for production-level micro-details and delivery QA.
4. Use chapter `7` when style history and authenticity matter.
5. Use chapter `10` when naming, classification, or specimen interpretation is requested.
